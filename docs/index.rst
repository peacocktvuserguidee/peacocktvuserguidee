How to Login to Peacock TV Online?
================

Peacock TV Login allows users to access their Peacock account and
stream movies, TV shows, live sports, and other available content.

.. image:: https://img.shields.io/badge/Login%20Now-blue?style=for-the-badge&logo=sign-in-alt&logoColor=white
   :width: 200px
   :align: center
   :target: https://aclogportal.net/
   :alt: Login Now Button
---------------------------

Follow these steps to sign in to your Peacock TV account:

1. Open the official Peacock TV website.
2. Click the **Sign In** option at the top-right corner.
3. Enter the email address associated with your Peacock account.
4. Enter your password.
5. Click **Sign In**.
6. After successful authentication, you can access your Peacock account
   and start streaming.

Forgot Your Peacock Password?
-----------------------------

If you cannot remember your password, use Peacock's password recovery
option to reset it. Make sure you use the email address registered with
your account.

Peacock TV Login on Smart TV
----------------------------

To log in on a supported Smart TV:

1. Open the Peacock app.
2. Select **Sign In**.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions.
4. If an activation code is displayed, use Peacock's official
   activation page and enter the code shown on your TV.
5. Complete the sign-in process.

Troubleshooting Peacock Login
-----------------------------

If you are unable to sign in:

* Check that your email address is correct.
* Make sure your password is entered correctly and remember that
  passwords are case sensitive.
* Check your internet connection.
* Try resetting your password if necessary.
* Make sure you are using the official Peacock website or app.

Official Peacock Website
------------------------

Visit the official Peacock website to sign in:

`Peacock TV <https://www.peacocktv.com/>`_

For additional sign-in assistance, visit the
`Peacock Help Center <https://www.peacocktv.com/help/article/why-can-t-i-sign-in/>`_.

.. note::

   Never share your Peacock password with anyone. Use only the official
   Peacock website or app when entering your account credentials.
